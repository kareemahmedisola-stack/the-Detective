What's new in this edition
  • New: accessibility everywhere. In every game press Q for an audio compass, W for where you are, G for your goal, Y for a recap, E for the evidence board, K for high contrast, F for partner mode and ? for help.
  • New: one campaign. Press 5 to play all three missions in order, with story between them and a record of your results. Danger now shows as a pulsing red vignette, with film grain, scene cuts and cinematic bars at the big moments.
  • Rebuilt game: Siege of Ilesanmi is now a deep strategy game. The enemy brings rams, towers, sappers and boats, each with its own sound and its own counter. Build works, hunt a spy who is different every game, and choose your way to win. Headphones recommended.
  • New: Lagos Radio, a map with travel between places, and Heat with police checkpoints in the detective cases.
  • Menu music plays here and before every case begins.
  • Accuse the Chief and he slaps you, then the sad tune plays.
  • Name the wrong person: the sad tune. Make a right decision: applause.
  • Name the real culprit and they run into Lagos traffic. Catch them and the police ask: "Why are you running?"
  • New: animated people who walk, talk, blush and dance, plus a love story in each case. Your choices decide the ending.
  • Watch out for tricks: fall for one and you will feel the emotional damage.

Inside a case, press Alt+M or use the Menu button to come back here.
